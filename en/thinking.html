Thinking Medical as a method

It is not a set of skills but a learning: how to select the data, how to build a testable hypothesis, and how to know the time is right to close the diagnosis.

«The most dangerous moment in diagnosis is not the error, but early certainty.»

— From working notes
Stages

How the clinical mind works

Six fields, from first attention to final documentation.

Medical Thinking

A new, innovative theory that works to reform thinking and its methods at all levels: the public, curriculum designers, policy makers, and the media in health affairs.

Logic, Dialogue and Argumentation

Structured training in correcting inference and the power of dialogue, from theoretical rules to live applied argumentation, combining intellectual and argumentative tools with the most important issues that occupy first the general intelligentsia, then researchers, scholars, preachers and students of the sciences.

Language and Eloquence (Thinking and Expression)

When eloquence and expression change the power of thinking; the Arabic tongue is a tool of thinking before it is a tool of expression, and precision of phrase is the first precision of thought.

Verification and Renewal

It includes examining claims before adopting them, in thinking that dots every i and crosses every t.

Dawah and Renaissance

Dawah: introducing the One Who commands before obligating His commands, and the comprehensive methodology (faith first). Renaissance: practical investment in thirty known, renewed and innovative types of thinking in establishing renaissance thinking and spreading its skills throughout the Ummah, from the smallest worker who masters (click publishing) to the greatest specialized scholar who still needs further refinement in the skills of thought.

Afflictions of Thinking

Studying foolishness and patterns of mental error: how they arise, and how we guard against them in ourselves before others.
Independent field

Linguistic Thinking

The book 'Al-Fath fi al-Tafkir al-Lughawi' (The Opening in Linguistic Thinking): a revolutionary renewal in Arabic teaching curricula for native and non-native speakers.
