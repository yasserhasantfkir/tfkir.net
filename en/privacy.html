Privacy Policy

A brief and clear description of how data sent through this site is handled.

Last updated: 2026-10-17

1. What data this site collects

This is a static site with no database and no user accounts. You are not asked to log in, and your preferences are stored only in the browser's own memory (such as the light/dark theme).

2. Contact form

When the form is submitted, the data (name, email, phone if provided, subject, and message text) is sent to the site's email in order to respond, and is not used for any marketing purpose, nor shared with a third party.

  • A hidden field (honeypot) prevents automated messages and is answered silently.
  • You are not asked for any medical or confidential data. Please do not send it.

3. Cookies

The site uses no cookies for tracking or advertising. localStorage is used only to remember the display theme (light/dark), and you can clear it from your browser settings at any time.

4. External fonts

The site loads fonts from the Google Fonts service. When loading them, the provider receives your IP address like any ordinary network request. This can be prevented with a blocking extension in the browser.

5. Analytics tools

If analytics tools are added (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics), the nature and identity of the collected data will be explained here.

6. Medical content

Everything published on the site is a personal opinion for educational purposes; it does not replace direct medical consultation nor the judgement of the physician responsible for your case. In emergencies, do not wait for a reply from this site.

7. Your rights

You can at any time request the deletion or modification of your message, or object to the processing of your data. Write to tafkiryas@gmail.com and the request will be carried out.

8. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the site changes. Last updated: 2026-10-17.
