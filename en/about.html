Overview

Repairing thought is the key to every repair

An intellectual and educational platform led by Dr. Yasser Hassan: organised around a single central premise, namely that the problems of the individual, society, the Ummah and the world are solved when we repair thinking, strengthen its skills, and change its ways and structures.

The story in brief

The medical journey began with the question, not the certificate. This site remains a continuation of the same question in another form: what happens to a physician when he writes what he sees, and what happens to medicine when it is discussed openly?

Why do I write?

Because a case that is not written is lost, and an idea that is not tested remains a belief. Writing here is an extension of the clinic, not an escape from it.

What I offer

Lectures on clinical thinking, workshops on case writing, dialogues on medicine and philosophy, and occasionally intellectual consulting for a nascent project.

Clear boundaries

What is published here is a personal opinion for educational purposes; it does not replace direct medical consultation, and is not a substitute for the judgement of the physician responsible.
